# COURT---NMF---Player---Role---Discovery
This codebase studies how NCAA Division I basketball players develop and
of interpretable on-court roles and follows those role profiles over time.
The current manuscript introduces **COURT-NMF**—Contextual,
Opportunity-weighted, Role-Trajectory Non-negative Matrix Factorization. The
method accounts for unequal playing opportunities, conference-and-season
context, and player transfers while learning role prototypes and each player’s
membership in them. Its goal is to provide a more faithful, interpretable view
of player development, role specialization, and movement through the college
basketball role landscape. The repository also preserves UMAP + HDBSCAN and
direct NMF + K-means analyses as reproducible baselines and validates the
resulting role structure against external position labels.
